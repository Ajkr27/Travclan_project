BUSINESS ANALYST ASSESSMENT

📋 Executive Summary

Analysis of 30,000 hotel booking transactions across 10 US cities reveals a 20.2% cancellation rate and an additional 7.5% booking failure rate — putting a combined $196M+ in annualized booking value at risk. The Web channel dominates revenue ($423M), while Travel Agents post the highest cancellation rate (27.9%). Seasonal spikes in July–August (30%+ cancellations) and December–January demand targeted intervention. Standard rooms are disproportionately cancelled (23.3%) despite representing 55% of inventory. Mobile App checkout failures (10.9%) present a quick-win technical opportunity.

💡 KEY INSIGHTS & ROOT CAUSES
🔥

Summer Cancellation Spike

July (30.3%) and August (28.8%) show cancellation rates nearly double the baseline (~17%). Travelers book early but switch to better deals as summer approaches. Weather uncertainty for outdoor destinations also contributes.

CRITICAL RISK
📱

Mobile App Checkout Failures

The Mobile App has a 10.9% booking failure rate vs Web's 5.2% — a gap of 5.7 percentage points. This suggests payment gateway friction, session timeouts, or poor UX at checkout, causing revenue leakage.

QUICK FIX
🤝

Travel Agent Channel Underperforms

Travel Agents generate only $73M (10% of revenue) but have the highest cancellation rate (27.9%). This channel has lower commitment from end-customers and adds intermediary friction to the booking process.

REVIEW NEEDED
🛏️

Standard Room Cancellation Problem

Standard rooms (55% of inventory) have a 23.3% cancellation rate vs Deluxe's 16%. Price-sensitive customers use Standard rooms as "placeholder" bookings while they search for better deals.

POLICY CHANGE
⭐

5-Star Paradox

Despite commanding the highest average booking values, 5-star properties have the highest cancellation rate (21.3%). Customers may aspirationally book premium properties then downgrade when costs become real.

INVESTIGATE
💳

Bank Transfer = Commitment Signal

Bank Transfer users cancel less (19.2%) than PayPal (20.8%). The friction in bank transfers creates a natural commitment filter — customers who transfer are more serious about their booking.

OPPORTUNITY
✅ BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS
01

Non-Refundable Discount Tiers

Introduce a 10-15% discount for non-refundable bookings during Jul/Aug and Dec/Jan. This converts price-sensitive customers to committed bookings and reduces seasonal cancellation spikes.

⚡ Impact: HIGH | Timeline: 4 weeks
02

Fix Mobile App Checkout

Audit and fix Mobile App payment flow. Reducing failure rate from 10.9% to 6% (Web level) could recover ~$14M+ in annual booking value. Add payment retry logic and Apple/Google Pay.

⚡ Impact: HIGH | Timeline: 6–8 weeks
03

Travel Agent Strategy Review

Renegotiate contracts with Travel Agents to include cancellation penalties passed to agents. Alternatively, shift focus to direct Web/App acquisition with targeted SEO and paid campaigns.

📊 Impact: MEDIUM | Timeline: 3 months
04

Dynamic Pricing Engine

Implement real-time pricing adjustments based on demand, seasonality, and cancellation risk scores. Premium pricing during Jul–Aug, early-bird discounts for off-peak months (Nov, Apr).

⚡ Impact: HIGH | Timeline: 3–6 months
05

Loyalty & Repeat Booking Program

Launch a points-based loyalty program. Currently only ~20% of bookings appear to be repeat customers. A 10% increase in repeat bookings could add $30M+ in confirmed revenue annually.

⚡ Impact: HIGH | Timeline: 2–3 months
06

Upgrade Migration Strategy

Offer Deluxe rooms at a small premium to Standard bookers at checkout. Since Deluxe has 16% cancellation vs Standard's 23%, migrating even 20% of Standard bookers reduces churn significantly.

📊 Impact: MEDIUM | Timeline: 4–6 weeks
